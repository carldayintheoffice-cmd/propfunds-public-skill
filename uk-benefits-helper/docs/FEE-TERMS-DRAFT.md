# "Do it for me" service - fee terms (DRAFT)

> **Draft for the owner and a solicitor to review. Not legal advice and not
> ready to use.** The numbers in `rules/pricing.js` must match whatever is
> agreed here.

## Decisions for the owner

| Decision | Draft choice | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fee basis | **£299, or the first 3 months of new money, whichever is less** (owner decision) | Simple to say, and never more than a small award is worth |
| What counts as new money | New regular benefit payments plus regular Council Tax / rates reductions | Both are money the customer keeps every week |
| What is not counted | Back pay, one-off and winter payments | Lump sums would distort the 3-month figure |
| Several benefits awarded | One fee covers every claim we make for the customer | Avoids "fee per benefit" stacking |
| When charged | Once, after the customer receives the award letter | No win, no fee |
| How paid | Secure payment link (card or Direct Debit) sent after award | Nothing collected up front; no bank details on the phone |
| Hardship | Reduce or waive on request | Vulnerable customers; reputation |
| VAT | Fee includes VAT if we are VAT-registered | Price shown is the price paid |

## Plain-English terms shown to customers

1. The check is free. Claiming benefits is free. You can claim yourself or get
   free help from Age UK, Independent Age or Citizens Advice.
2. If you ask us to do it for you, we help you fill in the claim, you check and
   sign it, we send it and chase it up.
3. You pay nothing unless the claim is successful.
4. If it is, our fee is £299, or 3 months of your new money if that is less.
   You pay once, however many claims are approved, after your award letter
   arrives.
5. You can cancel within 14 days of signing up, free, for any reason. After
   that you can still cancel; if we have already sent the claim and it is later
   successful, the fee still applies.
6. If paying would cause you hardship, tell us and we will reduce or waive it.
7. The money is always paid by the government straight to you. We never handle
   your benefit payments.
8. We never ask for passwords, GOV.UK / Government Gateway logins, or bank
   details on the phone.
9. Every answer on the claim must be true. We will not help with a claim we
   think is wrong, and we may stop helping if we are asked to.
10. We keep your details securely, use them only for your claim, and delete
    them on request (subject to records we must keep by law).

## Questions for the solicitor

- Confirm the service is not a regulated claims management activity for these
  benefits (FCA "specified benefits" appear limited to industrial injuries
  benefit - Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Claims Management
  Activity) Order 2018).
- Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013: cancellation rights and the wording of
  the "fee still applies after the claim is sent" clause for a service started
  inside the 14 days.
- Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024: price transparency and
  fair treatment of vulnerable consumers.
- Whether a success fee can be enforced if the customer refuses to pay, and
  the debt collection approach (we suggest none beyond reminders for this
  customer group).
- Acting with a family member's help: consent and the claimant's own signature
  (we will not act as a DWP appointee for profit).
- UK GDPR: health and income data are special category / sensitive. ICO
  registration, privacy notice, data protection impact assessment, retention.
